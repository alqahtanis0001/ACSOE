# 52 — Triple-barrier labelling

**Owner:** C — Interface and models

**Phase:** 4. `research/labelling.py` is C under the permanent ownership map.

## Goal

`src/acsoe/research/labelling.py` turns a series of closed 15-minute candles into
triple-barrier labels: `target` at +3%, `stop` at −1.5%, `timeout` at 48 bars. The values
come from `config/default.yaml` under `barriers` and are never written into the code.

## Two rulings, made by the lead, because the obvious reading of the spec is silently wrong

Both are recorded here rather than left to the implementer, and both are flagged to the
operator as lead rulings that may be overturned.

**1. On a bar that touches both barriers, the label is `stop`.** An OHLC candle whose high
reaches the target and whose low reaches the stop does not say which came first, and the
archives carry no intra-bar sequence to recover it from. Labelling it `target` invents the
favourable ordering on exactly the bars where the market was most violent, and a model
trained on that learns an edge that does not exist. The pessimistic reading is the only one
that cannot flatter the strategy. Record how many labels were decided this way in the output,
because a slice where that count is large is a slice whose labels are mostly an assumption.

**2. The timeout barrier is a time, not a count of rows.** The archives contain only
intervals in which trades occurred, so 48 *existing rows* can span days across a quiet
period. The timeout is `decision_bar_ts + timeout_bars * interval_s`; the walk visits
whatever candles exist inside that window and stops at its end. Counting rows silently
stretches a 12-hour horizon into a multi-day one over precisely the gaps that
`research/historical.py` refuses to interpolate away, and the resulting label is a fabricated
outcome the model then learns from.

## Implementation

1. A labelling function over the frame `research/historical.py` produces. For each decision
   bar it walks forward within the timeout window and returns the first barrier touched, the
   timestamp and bar index at which it was touched, the touch price, and the return at that
   point. Prices are `Decimal`; the return is a float statistic.
2. **A decision bar whose timeout window extends past the end of the series is not labelled
   at all.** It is excluded, not labelled `timeout`. Labelling it `timeout` records an
   outcome that has not happened yet, which is invariant 10 with a different face.
3. Barrier prices are computed from the decision bar close: target `close * (1 + target_pct)`,
   stop `close * (1 - stop_pct)`, both exact `Decimal`.
4. A touch is `high >= target` or `low <= stop` on a bar **after** the decision bar. The
   decision bar itself is never inspected for a touch — that is look-ahead inside one bar.
5. The output carries, per row: pair, decision bar timestamp, close, target and stop prices,
   the label, the touch timestamp, bars elapsed, the touch price, whether the bar was
   ambiguous under ruling 1, and the count of candles actually present in the window.
6. `tests/research/test_labelling.py`, C lane.

## The hand-verified fixture

`tests/fixtures/labels_hand_verified.json` — **20 labels**, verified by hand against the real
candles and committed. Requirements:

- Drawn from the real archive, not synthesised. All three outcomes present, plus at least one
  ambiguous bar under ruling 1, at least one window containing a gap, and at least one bar
  near the end of the series that must be **excluded**.
- Each entry records the pair, the decision bar timestamp, the close, both barrier prices, the
  expected label, the expected touch timestamp and the expected bars elapsed.
- **Verified independently of the labeller.** Print the candle window, check the arithmetic
  against it by hand, and write the working into `docs/build-log/phase-4/c-interface.md` — not
  the result, the working, for at least three of the twenty. A fixture generated by running
  the labeller and saving its output proves the labeller equals itself.

## Rules this spec is held to

- **Every labelling test must be proven capable of failing**, with the exact mutation and the
  exact red message recorded in the build log. Name at least: `>=` to `>` on the target touch;
  target checked before stop reversed to stop before target on an unambiguous bar; the timeout
  counted in rows rather than in time; the end-of-series exclusion removed; the decision bar
  itself included in the touch scan.
- Labels use future data by definition and **may only exist in the training pipeline, never in
  the live path** — invariant 10. Nothing in `acsoe.engines`, `acsoe.core`, `acsoe.clients` or
  `acsoe.cli` may import this module, and nothing in this module may import from them.

## Scope Limits

- Do **not** train, fit, score or rank anything. Phase 5 owns models.
- Do **not** write the walk-forward splitter here — spec 53.
- Do **not** build candles, read raw recordings, or write into `data/`. The input is the frame
  the loader returns.
- Do **not** interpolate, forward-fill or resample a gap. There is deliberately no flag for it
  and there must not be one.
- Do **not** invent a barrier value, a horizon or a fourth outcome.
